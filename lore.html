Lore

In 2037, a war broke out between the United States of America, and some European countries. The US was losing the war and they needed to find more soldiers, but it was no match for the European forces. With this, the US needed stronger soldiers, so they found a bunch of researchers across the world, kidnapping them and forcing them to create a substance that could potentially make their soldiers stronger. They had to be quick, as time was starting to run out.

Chapter 1 — The Substance

After months of research and testing, they found a substance that could enhance the soldier's endurance, strength, speed, and much more. It was human █████, mixed with the █████ of other animals, with some substances. They first experimented the substance on a kidnapped human, and it worked, they kept testing on the human again and again and again..
