Rules

Rules
  • Respect everyone. Come on. Exceptions are accepted when it's in character.
  • No trolling. Please.
  • No metagaming. You can't use information outside of your character's knowledge.
  • Fear Roleplay. If you would be scared in this situation, your character should be too.
  • NO ERP.
  • No golden weapons or guns. Only head guards and guards can use them. The permitted guns of them are listed in the roles page.
  • No admin abuse.
  • When performing actions (such as attacking), you must ask the other party for PTA, PTA — Permission To Attack
    Permission to attack another player.     PTI, PTI — Permission To Injure
    Permission to injure another player.     PTHI, PTHI — Permission To Heavily Injure
    Permission to heavily injure another player.     PTKO, PTKO — Permission To Knock Out
    Permission to knock out another player.     PTCounter, PTCounter — Permission To Counter
    Permission to counter an attack, injury, or similar action.     PTK, PTK — Permission To Kill
    Permission to kill another player.     etc.
  • Do not intentionally escape containment without allowment from the host.
  • Common sense.
Punishments
  • Warnings may be issued for minor offenses.
  • Repeated violations may lead to kick and eventually to a roleplay ban.
  • Severe rule breaking may result in blacklists.
